# Asset provenance

The SVG files in this directory are web-native explanatory diagrams created for
the project page from the concepts and qualitative findings described in the
paper *When, Why, and How Do Diffusion Posterior Samplers Fail? A Finite-Sample
Lens*.

They are not figures from the paper, reproductions of paper figures, or
visualizations of experimental measurements. They contain no inferred or
fabricated numerical results. Their purpose is to explain the finite-sample
diagnostic workflow and organize the paper's failure modes for the web.

- `conceptual-flow.svg` summarizes the finite-sample reference and its use as a
  comparison target for approximate likelihood scores.
- `failure-taxonomy.svg` groups qualitative stressors and observed classes of
  failure without encoding effect size, prevalence, or benchmark performance.

Both assets are self-contained SVG, use text labels in addition to color, and
include an accessible title and description.
